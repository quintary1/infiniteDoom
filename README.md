# 🕹️ GRAVE ESCAPE 3D: ARCADE MANUAL
### Procedural Roguelike Raycaster Shooter

Welcome to the military catacombs of **Grave Escape 3D**. This manual contains critical operational information for surviving the procedural catacombs, managing your weapon arsenal, utilizing modular upgrades, and navigating hostile encounters.

---

## 📜 MISSION BRIEFING
Your objective is to descend deeper into the procedurally generated military catacombs. Each floor contains keycards, ammunition, health packs, and hostiles. Locate the elevator shaft on each level to descend deeper.

1. **Search**: Explore rooms to find red and blue keycards required to unlock security doors.
2. **Eliminate**: Defeat catacomb guards and ghouls.
3. **Upgrade**: Complete a floor to draft a stackable booster upgrade before descending.
4. **Survive**: Manage your ammunition and health pools to claim a spot on the leaderboards.

## 🔫 WEAPON ARSENAL

The military cabinet provides three core weapon configurations. Manage your ammunition carefully.

### 1. 🔫 Standard Pistol
* **Damage**: 35 HP
* **Ammo Cost**: 1 bullet per shot
* **Cycle Rate**: 350ms
* **Range**: Short-to-Mid (6.0 units base)
* **Summary**: A reliable, accurate starting weapon with a decent fire rate.

### 2. 🪓 Combat Shotgun
* **Damage**: 100 HP (20 HP per pellet x 5 pellets base)
* **Ammo Cost**: 2 bullets per shot
* **Cycle Rate**: 850ms
* **Range**: Short Range
* **Summary**: Devastating close-range blast with multi-pellet spread. Excellent for clearing tight doorway bottlenecks.

### 3. ⚙️ Heavy Minigun
* **Damage**: 25 HP
* **Ammo Cost**: 1 bullet per shot
* **Cycle Rate**: 120ms
* **Range**: Long Range (16.0 units base)
* **Summary**: Unleashes a rapid-fire stream of bullets. Melts high-health targets but drains your ammunition reserves extremely fast.

## 👾 ENEMIES (BESTIARY)

The catacombs are populated by three distinct hostile guards. Each requires different combat spacing.

* **Standard Guard**: Equipped with standard-issue rifles. They patrol hallways, alert nearby guards on sight, and shoot from mid-range with high base accuracy.
* **Fast Ghoul**: Agile, melee-focused aberrations. They sprint toward you at high speeds and attempt to scratch you at close range. Keep your distance or deploy the shotgun.
* **Heavy Elite**: Armored heavy gunners with massive health pools (`130+` HP). They charge slowly and fire high-damage 3-shot bursts. Duck behind walls or doors during their bursts to avoid heavy damage.
